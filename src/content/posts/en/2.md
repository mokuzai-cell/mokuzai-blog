---
title: "Amazed by Astro Islands"
description: "Delivering JavaScript only where it's needed"
created: 2026-05-13
---

I recently tried out "Astro Islands" (island architecture), which is the biggest feature of Astro.

By default, it renders pure, static HTML. Then, you can use directives like `client:load` to partially hydrate only the components that need dynamic interaction—like hamburger menus or carousels. This approach feels so clean and efficient.

Compared to the old way of "hydrating the entire page by default," the performance advantage here is absolutely massive.
